artvibe — Privacy Policy

Effective: August 27, 2026

artvibe ("the app") shows you the story behind the music you're listening to: artist and album background, credits, reviews, and upcoming shows. It is developed by Aleksei Verein, who is the data controller for the purposes of the GDPR (contact: tpom6oh+artvibe@gmail.com).

This policy explains what data the app touches, what actually leaves your device, and what we keep.

What the app reads on your device

To know what you're listening to, artvibe uses Android's Notification Access (Notification Listener) permission to read the media metadata of the currently playing track — the artist name, track title, album name, artwork, and playback state exposed by your music player. This works with any player (Spotify, YouTube Music, etc.).

The app reads only media-playback metadata. It does not read, store, or transmit the content of your other notifications (messages, emails, or anything else). Non-music playback (podcasts, videos) is filtered out and ignored.

Your listening history and cached track information are stored locally on your device and can be removed by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.

What leaves your device

1. Track lookups. To build the card, the app queries public music-data services directly from your device. They receive different things:

ServiceWhat it receives
MusicBrainzartist name, track title, album name
Wikipediathe artist, album or song name being looked up
Cover Art Archivea MusicBrainz identifier for the album — no names
CritiqueBrainza MusicBrainz identifier for the album — no names
Ticketmasterthe artist name only — no track or album

As with any request your phone makes, each one also carries your IP address, which those services see. We send no account, no device identifier, and nothing that links one lookup to another. The requests are ephemeral: we operate no server, so they are never collected, stored, or built into a history anywhere but on your own device. Each service is an independent controller and handles what it receives under its own privacy policy. These lookups are what the app is for, so they cannot be switched off separately; not granting Notification Access stops them entirely.

2. Anonymous usage analytics and crash reports — only if you agree. artvibe asks on first launch, with the switch off. If you leave it off, no analytics software is started, no identifier is created, and nothing in this section happens. If you turn it on, the app sends anonymous events to PostHog (hosted in the European Union), which we use to understand how the app is used and to fix what is broken. This includes:

  • an anonymized identifier: a one-way cryptographic hash (SHA-256) of your device's Android ID. Your actual Android ID never leaves the device, and the hash cannot be reversed into it;
  • usage events: which screens are viewed, whether track identification succeeded and how long it took, and taps on features (for example, expanding a story, following a credit, or opening a ticket link). These events do not include the names of tracks or artists you listen to;
  • crash reports: when the app crashes, the type of error and the line of our code it happened in. Error messages are deliberately excluded, because they can quote the track being processed;
  • basic device context: device model, OS version, app version, locale, timezone, and screen size.

3. Wrong-match reports — only when you tap "Not right?". Identifying a track from a title is imprecise, and the only way to fix a bad match is to see it. When you tap "Not right?", the app sends the artist, title and album your player reported, together with the match we got wrong and how confident we were.

This report is sent unlinkably. It carries no device identifier, no session, and none of the device context (model, OS, locale) attached to the events above — a freshly generated random id is used once and immediately discarded, so we cannot tell which reports came from the same person. It is a fact about a track, not a record about you. Nothing is sent unless analytics is switched on, and nothing is sent unless you tap the button.

Two limits, stated plainly rather than glossed over. First, the report goes out the moment you tap, so it lands close in time to your other activity; we hold no identifier connecting the two, but timing by itself carries some information, and the fewer people using the app the more it narrows down. Second, suppressing the IP address stops the report being stored against your address — it cannot stop our analytics provider's servers seeing the connection that delivers it, as any server does. The report is unlinkable in the data we hold, not unobservable.

You can turn this off at any time in Settings → Privacy & data. Turning it off stops all collection immediately and discards anything not yet sent.

We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, precise location, or any account information. The app has no accounts.

All network traffic uses encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS).

Your rights

If you are in the EU or UK you have the right to access, correct, erase, restrict, or object to the processing of your personal data, the right to data portability, and the right to withdraw consent at any time. You may also lodge a complaint with your national data protection authority.

Because the only identifier we hold is an anonymous hash, we cannot find your records from your name or email. Settings → Privacy & data shows the identifier for your device — include it in your request and we can act on it. Email tpom6oh+artvibe@gmail.com; we respond within 30 days.

Where analytics data is processed

Analytics data is stored and processed by PostHog on servers in the European Union, under a data processing agreement. PostHog acts only as our processor and not for its own purposes. Nothing else reaches a server of ours, because we operate none. The public music-data services listed above receive only a track query and are independent controllers under their own policies.

Data retention and deletion

Analytics data is stored by PostHog on our behalf. We do not set an automatic expiry on it, so assume it is kept for as long as the project exists.

You can have everything collected from your device erased at any time: email tpom6oh+artvibe@gmail.com with the identifier shown in Settings → Privacy & data, and we will delete those records within 30 days.

The one thing we cannot delete on request is a wrong-match report, because it carries nothing identifying the device that sent it — being unable to find your reports is precisely what makes them anonymous. They contain a track name and no trace of you.

Local data (history, caches) never reaches our servers and is deleted when you clear the app's storage or uninstall it.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.
