artvibe, developed by Aleksei Verein. Last updated: September 19, 2026.

How to request deletion

  1. Open artvibe and go to Settings → Privacy & data.
  2. Under Delete my usage data you will see an identifier — a long string of letters and numbers. Copy it.
  3. Email tpom6oh+artvibe@gmail.com with that identifier and ask for your data to be deleted.
  4. We delete the matching records within 30 days and reply to confirm.

The identifier is the only thing that connects your records to you. It is an anonymous one-way hash, not your name or email, so we cannot find your data without it — a request that does not include it is one we have no way to act on.

If the identifier is not shown, analytics was never switched on and there is nothing stored to delete.

What is not deleted, and why

Wrong-match reports. When you tap "Not right?" on a card, artvibe sends the track that was matched incorrectly. That report is sent with a single-use random identifier and no device information, so it cannot be traced to you — which also means we cannot find it to delete it. It contains a track name and nothing about you.

Data already on your own phone. Your listening history and cached track information never leave your device and are not ours to delete. Remove them by clearing artvibe's storage in Android settings, or by uninstalling the app.

Retention

We do not set an automatic expiry on analytics data, so assume it is kept for as long as the project exists unless you ask us to delete it. There is no additional retention period after a deletion request: the records are removed within 30 days and not archived elsewhere.

Stopping collection, which is different

Turning off Settings → Privacy & data → Share anonymous usage data stops any further collection immediately and discards anything not yet sent. It does not delete what was already collected — use the steps above for that.
